# JesusBaby Branding Guide

## Brand Identity

**Mission**: Reveal the light under the bushel through real stories of deliverance and hope in Jesus Christ.

---

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors
- **Indigo**: `#6366f1` - Primary brand color (faith, trust)
- **Cyan**: `#06b6d4` - Secondary accent (hope, light)
- **Amber/Gold**: `#fbbf24` - Accent (divine light, glory)

### Neutral Colors
- **Dark Slate**: `#0f172a` - Primary background
- **Slate**: `#1e293b` - Secondary background
- **Light Slate**: `#e2e8f0` - Text on dark backgrounds
- **White**: `#ffffff` - Primary text

### Semantic Colors
- **Success**: `#10b981` (Emerald)
- **Warning**: `#f59e0b` (Amber)
- **Error**: `#ef4444` (Red)

---

## Typography

### Font Family
- **Primary**: Geist (sans-serif) - Modern, clean, accessible
- **Monospace**: Geist Mono - Code and technical content

### Font Sizes
- **H1**: 48-72px, Bold
- **H2**: 36-48px, Bold
- **H3**: 24-32px, Semibold
- **Body**: 16-18px, Regular
- **Small**: 12-14px, Regular

---

## Logo & Icon Usage

### Favicon
- SVG format with gradient cross and light rays
- Represents faith, hope, and divine light
- Used in browser tabs and bookmarks

### OG Image
- 1200x630px for social media sharing
- Includes brand name, tagline, and key messaging
- Used for LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook previews

---

## Visual Elements

### Gradients
- **Primary Gradient**: Indigo → Cyan (left to right)
- **Background Gradient**: Dark Slate → Slate (top to bottom)

### Spacing
- Base unit: 4px
- Padding: 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px
- Margin: 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px

### Border Radius
- Small: 4px
- Medium: 8px
- Large: 16px
- Full: 9999px (pills, circles)

### Shadows
- Subtle: `0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)`
- Medium: `0 4px 6px rgba(0,0,0,0.1)`
- Large: `0 10px 15px rgba(0,0,0,0.1)`

---

## Tone & Voice

- **Authentic**: Real stories, genuine experiences
- **Hopeful**: Emphasize transformation and freedom
- **Spiritual**: Faith-centered messaging
- **Inclusive**: Welcome to all seeking deliverance
- **Empowering**: Emphasize Christ's power, not human weakness

---

## Social Media Guidelines

### LinkedIn
- Professional tone
- Focus on transformation and faith
- Include call-to-action for community engagement

### TikTok
- Casual, relatable tone
- Short, impactful testimonies
- Use trending sounds and formats
- Emphasize personal journey

### WhatsApp
- Conversational, warm tone
- Direct messaging
- Personal connection emphasis

## Accessibility

- Maintain WCAG AA contrast ratios
- Use semantic HTML
- Include alt text for all images
- Ensure keyboard navigation
- Test with screen readers
